Radio amateurs on the Yorkshire coast since 1922

We meet on alternate Monday evenings in Scarborough, and visitors are welcome, licensed or not.

Come along to a club night to see what amateur radio involves, get help with your licence, or just talk radio. It helps to let our secretary know you're coming, so someone can look out for you.

Getting your licence

The Foundation licence is the first step, and it's well within reach if you spend some time with the RSGB book Foundation Now! Exams are taken online, and you get your result as soon as you finish.

We no longer run courses ourselves, but Essex Hams run excellent free online training, and members at the club will help you through it.
