Privacy notice

This website does not use cookies for tracking, and it has no member accounts.

If you send us a message through the contact form, your name, email address, any callsign you give and your message are passed to the society's secretary by our form provider, Formspree. We use them only to reply to you and do not share them with anyone else. We delete messages once they are no longer needed.

To ask about or remove information we hold about you, contact the secretary.

Scarborough Amateur Radio Society is responsible for this site.
