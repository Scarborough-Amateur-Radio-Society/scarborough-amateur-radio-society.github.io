Morse practice

A free course in three levels, introducing one or two characters at a time. It was created for the club by Robert G0WHO and Ron G4GWC.

{%- comment -%} Audio players only appear for MP3s that exist in assets/audio/morse/, so the page works before all the audio has been uploaded. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign morse_audio = site.static_files | where_exp: "f", "f.path contains '/assets/audio/morse/'" %} {%- if morse_audio.size > 0 %}

Play each exercise and write down what you hear, then open the text to check your copy. Set your volume low to start with.

{%- else %}

The audio for these exercises is being restored. In the meantime, you can use the practice texts with a Morse app or a practice oscillator, and learn the alphabet at the bottom of the page.
